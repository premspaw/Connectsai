## 📋 Full Model Directory

### 1. Featured & Workhorse Gemini Models
- **Gemini 3.8 Flash**: Most intelligent workhorse model for coding, autonomous agent execution, and multimodal understanding.
- **Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite**: Budget-friendly everyday workhorse for low-latency, high-volume WhatsApp traffic and tool calling.
- **Gemini 3.8 Live**: Engineered for bidirectional real-time audio and live agent orchestration.
- **Gemini 3.7 Flash & 3.6 Flash**: Fast everyday drivers for multi-step workflows.
- **Gemini 2.5 Flash / 2.5 Pro**: Reliable foundation models with adaptive thinking and 1M+ token context.

### 2. Audio, Voice & Transcription
- **Gemini 3.5 Transcribe**: Audio transcription for customer voice notes and call recordings.
- **Gemini 3.8 Flash-Lite TTS**: Fast, cost-effective speech generation for phone agents.
- **Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS**: Flagship expressive text-to-speech with regional accent support.

### 3. Visual & Creative Generation
- **Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite Image (Nano Banana 2 Lite)**: Rapid asset creation for high-volume campaigns.
- **Gemini 3.1 Flash Image**: Turn-based conversational image editing with character consistency.
- **Gemini 3 Pro Image**: High-fidelity composition with legible text rendering (up to 14 reference inputs).
- **Veo 3.1 Fast / Veo 3.1 Lite**: Text-to-video and image-to-video generation for video marketing.

### 4. Open Models (Gemma Family)
- **Gemma 4**: Multimodal (text, image, audio) open model for reasoning and coding.
- **Gemma 3n**: Lightweight multimodal open model for edge execution in 140+ languages.
- **CodeGemma & MedGemma**: Specialized open models for coding and medical tasks.
